# Experimental lane

The experimental lane exists because the unusual part of Omnia must remain
allowed to fail.

Experiments may be strange, incomplete, visually aggressive, or commercially
wrong. They may challenge current architecture. They may not silently widen
runtime authority or public claims.

Every experiment records:

- the uncomfortable hypothesis;
- the smallest falsifiable artifact;
- the failure or kill criterion;
- the isolation boundary;
- what evidence would justify promotion;
- what must remain private.

An experiment is not a roadmap promise. A failed experiment is retained as a
short receipt when its failure teaches the system something.
